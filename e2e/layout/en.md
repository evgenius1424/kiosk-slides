# Moving the whole team to a **four-day week** without losing pace

We tried it for one quarter with two teams and measured output, response times and how people felt every week.

Output stayed the same, sick days dropped by a third, and nobody asked to go back.

- Same customer hours, covered in shifts
- Fridays off, meetings moved to Tuesday
- One shared calendar for cover
- Review again after six months

# Three steps to a **calmer week**

Start small, measure, then decide together with the people doing the work.

1. Pick two teams that volunteer and agree what to measure before anything changes.
2. Run the trial for one full quarter, with shifts that keep customer hours covered.
3. Share the results with everyone and vote on whether to keep it.
