# Most teams lose the room in the first three minutes because the slides **say too much at once**

We looked at 240 recorded meetings. When a slide held more than one idea, people stopped reading it and waited for the speaker to explain.

---

<!--
title: What we learned
label: Key points
footer: Bold, italic and links work in any text.
-->

# Three habits that **changed the meetings**

- One idea per slide, **big enough** to read from the back.
- Numbers get their own slide instead of a footnote.
- Every presentation ends with _one_ thing to remember.
- Drafts are shared as a link, not an attachment.

# Rolling it out in **four steps**

1. Pick one recurring meeting and rewrite its slides.
2. Measure how long the meeting takes for a month.
3. Share the before and after with the next team.
4. Make the short format the default template.
