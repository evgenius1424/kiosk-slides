# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Critique the work, not the person.
- No harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- No sharing of other people's private information.
- Respect that the maintainer makes the final call on the project's direction.

These rules apply in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone breaks these rules, contact the maintainer, [@evgenius1424](https://github.com/evgenius1424), privately. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove content, or ban anyone who does not follow this code.
